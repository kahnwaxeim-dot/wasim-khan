Human Rights Work in Mardan

Jamal Ashraf serves as the Chief Coordinator at Human Rights Watch (since July 2021) based in Rustam, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, and as the President of the Pakistan International Human Rights Organization.

His work focuses on protecting the rights of ordinary people — from raising voices against injustice to organizing community support for those who need it most.

Through his platform, he highlights cases that mainstream media often misses, giving a voice to the voiceless in Mardan and beyond.
